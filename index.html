One day, someone you love will tell a story for the last time.

You will not know it is the last time. They will not know it is the last time. LYLO helps you keep it — in their own words, in their own voice, while they are still here to tell it.

A memory, delivered — in their own voice
A favorite recipe. The story of a first car. The lesson they learned after failing at something. The way they met the love of their life.

When I was four, my mother passed away. I never got to sit with her and ask about her childhood, her dreams, the things she wanted to leave behind. Everything I know about her came secondhand.

I cannot get that back. But maybe your family still can. That is why I am building LYLO.

What LYLO keeps

  • 01 Stories, in their own voice
  • 02 Photos and the meaning behind them
  • 03 Recipes and family traditions
  • 04 Advice for children they may never meet
This is not about creating fake memories. It is about preserving what is real — under your family control, kept for the people who come after.
